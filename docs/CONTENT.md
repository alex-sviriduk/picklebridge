# Coaching content and rule references

The sport translations, drill scripts and priority weights are original authored guidance for this portfolio MVP. They have not been independently validated and do not guarantee improvement.

Rules-related concepts were checked against the [USA Pickleball rules summary](https://usapickleball.org/rules/summary/) and [USA Pickleball beginner guide](https://usapickleball.org/pickleball-skills/level-one/how-to-play-pickleball/): the serve/return two-bounce requirement, the non-volley zone and volley momentum restrictions, and distinct serve mechanics. Consult the [official rulebook](https://usapickleball.org/rules/) for current complete rules, especially before league or tournament play.

The coaching UI provides compact reminders, not a replacement for the rulebook. A volley is a ball struck before it bounces. A dink is a soft short shot typically played after a bounce near the kitchen. A third-shot drop aims to descend into the opposing kitchen and help the serving team advance. A reset absorbs incoming pace to create a softer next exchange.

Tennis transfer is the richest pathway. Other sport pathways are deliberately introductory. Transfer categories are qualitative descriptions, not measured conversion rates or predictions. All dashboard totals are computed from the current local player's saved sessions; no adoption, performance or improvement metrics are fabricated.
